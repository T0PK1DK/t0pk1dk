Who I am

I take freelance work from people who want an app, a game, or a site in the world. You bring the need. I build the thing.

Some work stays private. Some is live, like Actually Worth It. I don’t invent clients, awards, or screenshots to dress that up.

How I work

  • Ship, don’t theater. Scope it, build it, put it where people can use it.
  • Private means private. If a repo isn’t public, you won’t see a fake demo here.
  • Clients, not job boards. This site is for people hiring freelance help — not a recruiting funnel.

What I build

Apps with a job to do. Games with a loop you can feel. Sites that look designed on a phone and go live on Cloudflare Pages.

If you want a long case study with invented metrics, this is the wrong page. If you want someone who will make the product, you’re in the right place.
